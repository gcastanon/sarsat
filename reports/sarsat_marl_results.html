100-Agent: Best RL Approach

MAPPO, credit_mix 0.5, slot-mixture head is the best RL row: mean 0.898 over 16 seeds (16/16 beat Greedy-Beam), within 0.006 of Coop-Plan's 0.904. Five independent runs (three seeds, a wider observation, a fine-tune) all land at 0.894–0.898: level with Coop-Plan within noise, not above it. Held-out validation seeds 2000–2015 agree: 0.909 vs Coop-Plan 0.914, beating it on 2 of 16.

System

  • MAPPO (rec_mappo, MAPX) with shared parameters over 100 agents
  • CoopSarSat observation (ranked beam-value slots + cluster look-ahead)
  • Slot-mixture anchored head; credit_mix 0.5
  • 32 envs × 180-step rollouts, 4 epochs × 4 minibatches
  • Clip 0.1, gamma 0.995, lambda 0.95
  • Actor 3e-4 / critic 5e-4, linear LR decay
  • GRU 128, recurrent chunks of 30

What it learned

  • Rations battery toward hotspots rather than spending it all on background
  • Learns beam-aware pointing and side/sense choices, not just “aim somewhere”

What remains

  • Same-step duplicate captures ≈ 1.4% of the return (two agents take one beam)
  • Battery still ends episodes partly unspent

100-Agent: Observation, Action and Critic

Observation

  • 4 slots × 8: valid, incidence, squint, side (action encoding), beam value, scarcity-weighted value, team future accesses, contention
  • 16 × 2 look-ahead: remaining hotspot value reachable, raw and scarcity-weighted
  • 5 self: battery, can-sense, time remaining, visible value, visible count
  • All features log-squashed to roughly [-1, 1] / [0, 1]

Action

  • 4 slots: incidence [-1, 1], squint [-1, 1], side {0, 1}, sense {0, 1}
  • Unchanged from the base environment's action space

Critic input

  • Own view (69) + team summary (64): time remaining; battery mean & 10/50/90 percentiles; fraction able to sense
  • Remaining hotspot & background value
  • 40 sorted per-cluster remaining fractions
  • 16-step team-mean look-ahead
  • Size independent of N — 133 vs 9,600 for a joint-observation critic

100-Agent: Reward Shaping

  • The environment reward is untouched: shared team reward = priority newly imaged
  • reward_scale × 10 for value-loss conditioning (returns O(1–10); fraction_imaged still logs the true return)
  • credit_mix 0.5: ri = 0.5 · team + 0.5 · N · own_sharei; a target hit by k beams splits its value evenly; mean over agents is still the team reward
  • Why: a background target is 1/26,000 of the return — invisible in a 100-agent shared reward. With pure team reward MAPPO reaches 0.847 and IPPO only ≈ 0.74
  • This is credit assignment, not potential-based shaping: no new term rewards anything the team reward does not
  • No other reward terms

200-Agent: Best RL Approach

MAPPO, credit_mix 0.25, gamma 0.998 / lambda 0.98 is the best RL row: mean 0.649 over 16 seeds (16/16 beat Greedy-Beam), still 0.021 below Coop-Plan's 0.670 (97% of it) and +0.210 over Greedy-Beam; Solo-Plan, which plans as far ahead alone, gains nothing over Greedy-Beam here.

System

  • MAPPO, credit_mix 0.25, gamma 0.998 / lambda 0.98 — same shared-parameter recurrent system as the 100-agent recipe
  • WindowedCoopSarSat observation (9-wide slots; cluster look-ahead gated on window)
  • credit_mix 0.25
  • 12 envs × 180-step rollouts (16 for the first two variants); GAE horizon lengthened to gamma 0.998 / lambda 0.98
  • Otherwise the 100-agent PPO recipe (clip 0.1, 4 × 4, actor 3e-4 / critic 5e-4 with decay, GRU 128)

What it learned

  • Coordinated timing: which satellite must spend charge on the event only it can reach before its window closes

What remains

  • Plateau: five variants (IPPO/MAPPO, credit_mix 0.1–0.5, look-ahead 16/48, GAE horizon 18/67 steps) all land at 0.641–0.649 vs Coop-Plan's 0.670
  • Battery at window-open averages 0.37 vs Coop-Plan's 0.77 — the policy is not rationing far enough ahead of the event
  • Duplicate captures: 38 priority units vs Coop-Plan's 3.5
